About the game

Movement is everything

Geopals don't fight, they advance. Abilities push you forward, knock rivals back, and lock down the spaces in between.

The board changes

Terraform cards rewrite regions mid-race. A Geopal that was stranded can suddenly be at home — and at full speed.

Spend to accelerate

Berries pay for active abilities. Every burst of speed costs you something you might want later.

Nine regions

Deck building basics

Runners, Supporters, Chasers and All Rounders.

How to play

Every Geopal has a Style and a Stage, and a passive ability that's always on plus an active ability you pay Berries to use.

Styles

Runner covers ground. Chaser hunts down the leader. Supporter boosts the team. All Rounder does a bit of each.

Stages

Basic → Stage 1 → Stage 2, with a handful of Legends beyond that.

Rarity

C, B, A and S — plus Infinity for the rarest pulls. Many cards also come in Holo.
