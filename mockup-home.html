Trading Card Game

The world runs on one Orb, and it was broken.

Long before the race, a Geopal called Mallathork decided the Geoverse should answer to him. Controlling Geopals one at a time was never going to be enough, so he went for the two things everything else drew on. He trapped the Good-Being in another dimension, then set Geopals to mine the Core Orb and grew stronger as it weakened.

The Chasers stopped him. They could not destroy him. He was sealed inside the divided husks of the Orb he had drained, and those fragments became the Guardian Orbs the eight regions keep to this day. Every Geopal you play comes from one of them.

Sealed is not destroyed. He was contained, not killed.

How it plays

Geopals do not fight. They advance.
Abilities push you forward, hold rivals back, and lock down the spaces in between. First to arrive wins.
The ground changes underneath you
Terraform cards rewrite a region mid-race. A Geopal that was stranded is suddenly at home, and at full speed.
Every burst of speed costs
Berries pay for active abilities. Spending now is spending something you may want later.
Some Geopals Awaken
A Basic Geopal can become something else again. Stage 2 is not simply a bigger version of what you started with.
